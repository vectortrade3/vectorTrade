How the Offline-to-Online Trade Selection Protocol Works

1 Offline Scanning & Ranking

The offline scanner runs at market close (4:00 PM), processes 500+ NSE symbols with XGBoost ML models, and uploads candidate setups to MongoDB Atlas signals_watchlist. Candidates are sorted with the highest AI Expectancy (+R) and profit potential on top.

2 1% Mathematical Risk Sizing

Every candidate order is sized using:
Qty = floor(1% Capital / (Buy Trigger - SL))
This guarantees that even if a trade hits its stop loss, the loss cannot exceed your 1% risk threshold (e.g., ₹1,000 max loss on a ₹1 Lakh account).

3 Capital Budget Confirmation

The engine walks down the ranked list and commits capital until the available balance is exhausted. Confirmed orders are staged in MongoDB Atlas staged_orders for automatic 09:15 AM execution. Setups that exceed the budget wait on the waitlist.
